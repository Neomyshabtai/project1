Homemade Glazed Doughnuts 🍩

Learn how to make yeast-risen doughnuts that are light, airy, and coated in a simple, sweet glaze.

⏱️ 45 min prep 🔥 15 min fry 👤 Makes 12 doughnuts

Ingredients 🥣

  • 3/4 cup warm milk
  • 1 packet active dry yeast
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs
  • 4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, softened

Instructions 📝

  1. Activate Yeast: Mix milk, yeast, and 1 tsp of sugar. Let stand for 5 minutes.
  2. Mix Dough: Combine the yeast mixture, remaining sugar, vanilla, eggs, flour, and salt. Mix until a shaggy dough forms.
  3. Add Butter: Knead in the softened butter until the dough is smooth and elastic.
  4. First Rise: Place in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise for 1-1.5 hours until doubled in size.
  5. Shape & Fry: Roll out dough, cut into rings, and let rise for another 30 minutes. Fry in oil at 180°C until golden brown.
  6. Glaze: Dip warm doughnuts into a simple powdered sugar glaze and serve immediately.

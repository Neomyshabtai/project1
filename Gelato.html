Homemade Italian Gelato 🍨

We found the perfect Italian Gelato recipe! This homemade version delivers a smooth, velvety texture and intense, fresh flavor.

⏱️ 30 min prep ❄️ 4 hours freeze 👤 Serves 6

Ingredients 🥣

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 1 vanilla bean (or 1 tsp vanilla extract)
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions 📝

  1. Heat Base: Combine milk, cream, sugar, and salt in a saucepan. Bring to a simmer, stirring constantly. Remove from heat.
  2. Temper Yolks: Whisk egg yolks in a separate bowl. Slowly pour about 1/2 cup of the warm milk mixture into the yolks while whisking constantly.
  3. Cook: Pour the yolk mixture back into the saucepan. Cook over low heat, stirring until the mixture thickens slightly and coats the back of a spoon (about $80^\circ\text{C}$). Do NOT boil.
  4. Chill: Strain the mixture and stir in vanilla. Chill completely in the refrigerator (at least 4 hours, preferably overnight).
  5. Churn: Churn the gelato in an ice cream maker according to the manufacturer's instructions. Freeze until firm.

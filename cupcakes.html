Vanilla Bean Cupcakes 🧁

These perfectly fluffy vanilla cakes, crowned with a light, airy vanilla bean buttercream, are a delightful classic for any sweet occasion.

⏱️ 20 min prep 🔥 20 min bake 👤 Makes 12 cupcakes

Ingredients 🥣

  • 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1.5 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup milk

Instructions 📝

  1. Preheat & Prep: Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F) and line a muffin tin with paper liners.
  2. Dry Mix: Whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt in a bowl. Set aside.
  3. Cream: Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Beat in eggs one at a time, then add vanilla extract.
  4. Combine: Gradually add the dry mixture alternating with the milk, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.
  5. Bake: Fill liners two-thirds full. Bake for 18–20 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted comes out clean. Cool completely before frosting.

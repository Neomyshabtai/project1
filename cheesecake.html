Classic New York Cheesecake 🧀

A creamy, rich, and dense classic cheesecake with a buttery graham cracker crust. The perfect dessert for any occasion!

⏱️ 30 min prep 🔥 60 min bake 👤 Serves 10

Ingredients 🥣

  • Crust: 1.5 cups graham cracker crumbs, 1/4 cup sugar, 6 tbsp melted butter.
  • Filling: 4 packages cream cheese (softened), 1.5 cups sugar, 1/4 cup sour cream, 1 tsp vanilla extract, 4 large eggs.

Instructions 📝

  1. Crust: Preheat oven to $175^\circ\text{C}$ ($350^\circ\text{F}$). Combine crust ingredients and press firmly into the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan. Bake for 10 minutes.
  2. Filling: Beat cream cheese, sugar, sour cream, and vanilla until smooth and creamy. Beat in eggs one at a time until just combined.
  3. Bake: Pour the filling over the crust. Bake for 55-65 minutes until the edges are set but the center is slightly wobbly.
  4. Cool: Turn off oven and leave cake inside with the door slightly ajar for 1 hour. Remove and cool completely on a wire rack. Refrigerate overnight before serving.
